# Call to Action: ATF Proposed Rule “Selecting Biological Sex on ATF Forms”

**We’ll keep this one brief: the feds are attacking trans folks’ right to own firearms.**

This is not the first time that transgender people’s Second Amendment rights have come under threat from the Trump administration, but this is several steps beyond the usual transphobic rhetoric. 

In the ATF’s own words:
> This proposed rule would amend the language of 27 CFR 478.21, 479.21, and 555.21 to explain that “sex” as required by any ATF form (1) means an individual's immutable biological classification as either male or female, and (2) is not a synonym for and does not include the concept of gender identity.

**This cannot be interpreted as anything but an attack on transgender folks.**
Codifying a legal requirement to select one’s “biological sex” when completing ATF paperwork effectively means trans gun buyers will either need to commit a felony by falsifying their purchasing forms, or not purchase firearms commercially at all. At best, this new rule would be yet another example of gender essentialist transphobia ensconced in federal regulations, not to mention introducing more opportunities for error and misfiling on both the applicant and federal ends of the process. At worst, it is the first step in disarming and subjugating an already oppressed minority group.

Those are the broad strokes. Others have already written more extensively and eloquently on the matter, so check out [Trans News Network’s article on the subject](https://transnews.network/p/trump-atf-takes-aim-at-trans-gun-rights), including an interview with the inimitable [Tacticool Girlfriend](https://linktr.ee/tacticoolgf)
